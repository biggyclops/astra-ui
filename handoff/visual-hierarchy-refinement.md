# Visual Hierarchy Refinement

Date: 2026-05-09
Branch: `feature/astra-cinematic-shell`

## Focus
The Astra shell was refined to improve spacing, panel balance, visual rhythm, breathing room, and conversation readability without adding features or changing the identity.

## What Changed
- Narrowed the outer shell and redistributed width toward the center conversation column.
- Reduced sidebar visual weight and tightened its internal spacing.
- Lightened the context panel so it reads as supporting structure, not a competing focal point.
- Increased message cadence spacing and softened message chrome.
- Reduced glow intensity across panels, atmosphere, and presence cues.
- Improved alignment consistency and the sense of calm separation between regions.

## Result
- The center conversation area now reads as the dominant focal point.
- The shell feels calmer, more premium, and less dashboard-like.
- The atmosphere remains present but no longer overwhelms the content.

## Deliberately Unchanged
- No backend systems were added.
- No auth work was added.
- No telemetry complexity was added.
- The Astra visual identity remained intact.
